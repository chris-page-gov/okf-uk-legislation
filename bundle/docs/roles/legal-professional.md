# Legal-professional guide

[Role guides](index.md) · [Authority and evidence](../../whole-law/docs/authority-and-evidence.md) ·
[Effects and enrichment](../effects-and-enrichment.md) ·
[Evaluation](../evaluation-and-quality.md)

1. Find the exact work and provision, then distinguish work, expression,
   manifestation and selected passage.
2. Inspect official effects separately from derived and model-assisted
   discovery relationships.
3. Check commencement, territorial extent, point-in-time version and unapplied
   effects. A current-text label is not proof of operative law everywhere.
4. Use the Whole-Law coverage view to identify when case law, retained-EU
   interpretation, procedural material or another source family is required.
5. Preserve proposition-level citations and unresolved adverse or missing
   material for independent review.

This prototype supports research and verification; it is not legal advice,
qualified practitioner assurance or a complete citator.
