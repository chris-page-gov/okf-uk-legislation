Researcher guide

Role guides · Getting started · Relationships · Source coverage

  1. Open the UK Legislation descriptor for legislation discovery, or the Whole-Law descriptor when the task may require another legal-source family.
  2. Filter by source class, jurisdiction, document type, temporal state, authority and freshness before opening individual records.
  3. Use model-assisted topics and entities to discover candidates only.
  4. Confirm identity and every material proposition against the controlling source and record the selected passage, version, retrieval time and source identifier.
  5. Record inaccessible or missing evidence as a limitation; do not turn catalogue absence into a legal conclusion.

The official legislation data documentation defines the source service's representations and access patterns.

Independent public prototype. Verify legal effect and current text against the cited official source. Model-assisted discovery metadata is not official legal classification or legal advice.
