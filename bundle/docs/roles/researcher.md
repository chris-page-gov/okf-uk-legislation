# Researcher guide

[Role guides](index.md) · [Getting started](../getting-started.md) ·
[Relationships](../relationships.md) · [Source coverage](../source-coverage.md)

1. Open the [UK Legislation descriptor](https://chris-page-gov.github.io/okf-uk-legislation/okf-explorer.json)
   for legislation discovery, or the
   [Whole-Law descriptor](https://chris-page-gov.github.io/okf-uk-legislation/whole-law/okf-explorer.json)
   when the task may require another legal-source family.
2. Filter by source class, jurisdiction, document type, temporal state,
   authority and freshness before opening individual records.
3. Use model-assisted topics and entities to discover candidates only.
4. Confirm identity and every material proposition against the controlling
   source and record the selected passage, version, retrieval time and source
   identifier.
5. Record inaccessible or missing evidence as a limitation; do not turn
   catalogue absence into a legal conclusion.

The [official legislation data documentation](https://legislation.github.io/data-documentation/)
defines the source service's representations and access patterns.
