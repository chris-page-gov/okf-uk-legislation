Legal-professional guide

Role guides · Authority and evidence · Effects and enrichment · Evaluation

  1. Find the exact work and provision, then distinguish work, expression, manifestation and selected passage.
  2. Inspect official effects separately from derived and model-assisted discovery relationships.
  3. Check commencement, territorial extent, point-in-time version and unapplied effects. A current-text label is not proof of operative law everywhere.
  4. Use the Whole-Law coverage view to identify when case law, retained-EU interpretation, procedural material or another source family is required.
  5. Preserve proposition-level citations and unresolved adverse or missing material for independent review.
