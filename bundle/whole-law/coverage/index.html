Coverage and source constraints

The federation distinguishes a source record being catalogued from a corpus being ingested or complete. Exact denominators and access states are available in coverage.json; legal, fair-use, authentication, availability and hosting constraints are in source-constraint-ledger.json.

Restricted or unavailable sources remain visible. Their content is not fabricated and authentication is not bypassed.

Independent public prototype. Verify legal effect and current text against the cited official source. Model-assisted discovery metadata is not official legal classification or legal advice.
