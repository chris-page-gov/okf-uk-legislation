UK Whole-Law OKF

The UK Whole-Law OKF is a federated, evidence-led map of the sources needed to research UK law. It preserves the bounded UK Legislation OKF as its first implemented source-family bundle and records the coverage, authority, access and assurance state of every additional family.

The federation is a prototype discovery and research aid. It is not legal advice, a claim that every source is complete, or a substitute for checking the controlling source, jurisdiction, date, extent, version and subsequent treatment.

Published release

This publication is part of Legislation/Whole-Law v0.4.0, published on 3 November 2026. It uses the OKF 0.2 specification. Its source-access snapshot is dated 25 July 2026; the implemented Legislation child's catalogue snapshot is dated 11 July 2026.

The release version and the OKF specification version are independent: this is bundle release v0.4.0, authored to the OKF 0.2 profile. The source snapshots remain dated July 2026; publishing the corrected semantics and interfaces in November does not imply that upstream source data was refreshed.
