Point-in-time and historical research

Reconstruct the law and official materials as at a specified date.

Required authority

binding-authority, history

Evidence

Dated expressions, amendment chronology, archived official material and source hashes.

Currency

Exact historical cut-off; later material segregated.

Definition of success

A reproducible dated state and change chronology are supplied.

Failure modes

  • anachronism
  • current consolidation substituted for historical text
  • archive date mistaken for legal date

Independent public prototype. Verify legal effect and current text against the cited official source. Model-assisted discovery metadata is not official legal classification or legal advice.
