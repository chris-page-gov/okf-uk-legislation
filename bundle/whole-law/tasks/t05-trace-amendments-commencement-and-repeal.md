# Trace amendments, commencement and repeal

Identify effects made/received and whether they are applied, pending or spent.

## Required authority

binding-authority

## Evidence

Official effect feeds, amending instruments/provisions, commencement orders and selected passages.

## Currency

Live verification at answer time.

## Definition of success

Bidirectional change graph and current application status are evidenced.

## Failure modes

- absence of recorded effect treated as no effect
- prospective effect treated as operative
- repeal savings omitted
