# Construct a chronology

Order enactment, commencement, litigation, guidance, decisions and implementation events.

## Required authority

binding-authority, history, official-guidance

## Evidence

Typed dated events with source identity, event date and publication/retrieval date separated.

## Currency

Depends on task; current chronologies checked live.

## Definition of success

A typed, source-backed chronology exposes gaps and conflicting dates.

## Failure modes

- publication date confused with effective date
- events silently omitted
- time zones/version ambiguity
