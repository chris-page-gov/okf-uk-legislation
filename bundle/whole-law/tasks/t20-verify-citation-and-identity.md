# Verify citation and identity

Resolve citations, aliases, duplicate records, versions and successor identities.

## Required authority

binding-authority, history, legal-service-information

## Evidence

Source-native identifiers, exact/qualified mappings, issuer and temporal identity.

## Currency

Identity mapping versioned; live where institutions/citations evolve.

## Definition of success

Identity decision has mapping type, confidence, evidence and unresolved conflicts.

## Failure modes

- fuzzy match asserted as sameAs
- parallel citations collapsed
- wrong version joined
