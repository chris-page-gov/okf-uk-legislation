# Produce accessible public explanation

Explain rights, duties or process accurately in plain language and accessible formats.

## Required authority

binding-authority, official-guidance, legal-service-information

## Evidence

Authoritative passages plus official service guidance, with simplifications/limits labelled.

## Currency

Live for current rights/process.

## Definition of success

Explanation is accurate, scoped, readable, accessible and links to authoritative evidence.

## Failure modes

- exception omitted
- advice presented as certainty
- accessibility/translation errors
