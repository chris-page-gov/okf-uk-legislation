# Draft or check legal instruments

Check form, enabling power, competence, cross-references, commencement and consistency.

## Required authority

binding-authority, history, procedural-material

## Evidence

## Currency

Live at drafting/clearance milestones.

## Definition of success

## Failure modes

- defective cross-reference
- extent/commencement mismatch
