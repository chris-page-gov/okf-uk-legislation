# Apply rules to facts

Structure legal analysis against supplied facts without inventing missing facts.

## Required authority

binding-authority, persuasive-authority

## Evidence

Controlling text/cases, issue-element mapping, stated factual assumptions and uncertainty.

## Currency

Live for current advice; professional review required.

## Definition of success

Elements, exceptions, burdens and missing facts are mapped, with escalation.

## Failure modes

- facts invented
- legal conclusion overconfident
- discretion treated as rule
