# Quantitative or corpus analysis

Run reproducible analysis over a declared source population.

## Required authority

binding-authority, history, contextual-material

## Evidence

Licensed corpus, denominator, extraction rules, code/version, missingness and uncertainty.

## Currency

Snapshot date fixed and disclosed.

## Definition of success

Analysis is reproducible and limitations follow from an auditable coverage ledger.

## Failure modes

- licence breach
- selection bias
- database absence treated as real-world absence
