# Compare jurisdictions

Compare materially equivalent rules, remedies or procedures across UK legal systems or international sources.

## Required authority

binding-authority, procedural-material

## Evidence

Separate authoritative sources per jurisdiction with explicit non-equivalence.

## Currency

Same effective date across compared systems.

## Definition of success

Differences, commonalities, dates and institutional contexts are separately sourced.

## Failure modes

- English rule generalised to UK
- same label assumed same concept
- asynchronous reform ignored
