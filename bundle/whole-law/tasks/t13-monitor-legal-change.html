Monitor legal change

Detect, classify and route new or changed legal materials.

Required authority

binding-authority, procedural-material, delegated-administrative-rules, official-guidance

Evidence

Official feeds/logs, hashes, version differences and impact classification.

Currency

Source-specific service-level objective; critical sources daily or event-driven.

Definition of success

Change event is detected, typed, evidenced, impact-assessed and reviewable.

Failure modes

  • silent source deletion
  • publication event missed
  • cosmetic change escalated as legal change

Independent public prototype. Verify legal effect and current text against the cited official source. Model-assisted discovery metadata is not official legal classification or legal advice.
