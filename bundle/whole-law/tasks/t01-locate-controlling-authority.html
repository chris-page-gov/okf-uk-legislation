Locate controlling authority

Identify the highest relevant binding source and adverse authority, not merely a topical document.

Required authority

binding-authority

Evidence

Official work/case/rule identity plus selected passage and court/institutional competence.

Currency

Live verification at answer time for current advice; historical date fixed for retrospective research.

Definition of success

Controlling authority is identified, hierarchy and jurisdiction are stated, and every material proposition has a pinpoint.

Failure modes

  • topical but non-controlling source
  • later appeal or higher authority missed
  • guidance presented as law
