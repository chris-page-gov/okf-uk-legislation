# Identify judicial interpretation and treatment

Find cases interpreting a provision/proposition and classify treatment.

## Required authority

binding-authority, persuasive-authority

## Evidence

Official judgments, cited passage, court hierarchy, later treatment and appeal status.

## Currency

Live citator/treatment check before reliance.

## Definition of success

Treatment verbs are passage-evidenced and hierarchy/date qualified.

## Failure modes

- citation occurrence treated as approval
- headnote substituted for judgment
- later reversal missed
