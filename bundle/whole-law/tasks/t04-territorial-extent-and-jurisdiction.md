# Territorial extent and jurisdiction

Determine where and to whom a source applies and which institution has competence.

## Required authority

binding-authority, legal-service-information

## Evidence

Extent metadata, devolution/competence provisions, court or regulator jurisdiction sources.

## Currency

Live for current competence; exact for historical jurisdiction.

## Definition of success

Territory, legal system, institutional competence and exceptions are explicit.

## Failure modes

- UK-wide assumption
- extent confused with application
- court/body identity conflated
