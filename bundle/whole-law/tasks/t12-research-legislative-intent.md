# Research legislative intent

Trace Bill versions, debates, explanatory material, reports and implementation history.

## Required authority

history, binding-authority

## Evidence

Official Bill/stage documents, Hansard passages, explanatory notes and enacted text.

## Currency

Historical source set fixed; later interpretation segregated.

## Definition of success

History is connected to enacted provisions without overstating interpretive weight.

## Failure modes

- ministerial statement treated as law
- wrong Bill version
- selective quotation
