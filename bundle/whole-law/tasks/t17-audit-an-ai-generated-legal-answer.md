# Audit an AI-generated legal answer

Verify authority, passage, currency, jurisdiction, completeness and uncertainty.

## Required authority

binding-authority, persuasive-authority, procedural-material

## Evidence

Proposition-to-citation ledger, source hashes, version context and coverage declaration.

## Currency

Re-verify all material current-law propositions.

## Definition of success

Every material proposition is supported, classified and risk-rated; hard failures cap the answer.

## Failure modes

- invented citation
- citation does not support proposition
- hidden retrieval gap
