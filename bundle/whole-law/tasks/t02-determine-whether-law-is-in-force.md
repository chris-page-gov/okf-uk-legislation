# Determine whether law is in force

Establish operative status for the relevant date, territory, person and facts.

## Required authority

binding-authority

## Evidence

Versioned legislation, commencement/effect records, extent and amendments with live official verification.

## Currency

Zero tolerance for stale current-law answers.

## Definition of success

Answer states the relevant version, commencement, extent, amendments, savings and uncertainty.

## Failure modes

- latest text confused with in-force text
- unapplied effect ignored
- commencement or savings omitted
