Whole-Law governance and release assurance

The federation is released through a fail-closed lifecycle: draft → candidate → validated → rc → published. A requirement is not passed because it has merely been accepted; it needs linked implementation and validation evidence, or an explicit approved deferral or external block.

Model-assisted discovery assertions are derived metadata, not official legal classification or legal advice. Consequential legal use remains subject to qualified review against controlling, current source material.

Paid execution is prohibited until an explicit API-key choice and a separately recorded ready preflight. The credential-free policy fixes strict generator, reviewer, selection, attempt, cache, pricing, hard-cap and final-run contracts without assuming that any named model is available.
