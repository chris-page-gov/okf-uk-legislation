# Whole-Law governance and release assurance

The federation is released through a fail-closed lifecycle:
`draft → candidate → validated → rc → published`. A requirement is not passed
because it has merely been accepted; it needs linked implementation and
validation evidence, or an explicit approved deferral or external block.

classification or legal advice. Consequential legal use remains subject to
qualified review against controlling, current source material.
