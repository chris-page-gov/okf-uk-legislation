BAILII case-law database

Valuable free secondary publication; not the official publisher for most records and coverage is uneven.

Authority and coverage

  • Owner: British and Irish Legal Information Institute
  • Authority: persuasive-authority, contextual-material
  • Source classes: SC09, SC10, SC12, SC35, SC36
  • Jurisdictions: United Kingdom, Ireland, European/international collections
  • Coverage status: documented but inaccessible
  • Denominator: Database listings and collection pages; BAILII warns that it cannot guarantee completeness or accuracy.

Access

  • unavailable BAILII home/about — tested 2026-07-25

Rights and constraints

  • Licence: BAILII terms and document copyright; automated reuse requires specific review.
  • Fair use/rate/robots: Respect robots/terms; do not use as a silent bulk fallback.

Known limitations

  • Not official for most cases; missing/corrected records and duplicate identities are possible; tested access failed.
