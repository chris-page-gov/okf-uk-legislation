Official law reports and ICLR platform

Authorised law reports are highly significant for citation practice; the reporter/editorial apparatus is not an official government source and access is partly commercial.

Authority and coverage

  • Owner: Incorporated Council of Law Reporting for England and Wales
  • Authority: contextual-material, persuasive-authority
  • Source classes: SC12, SC13, SC36
  • Jurisdictions: England and Wales
  • Coverage status: licence-restricted
  • Denominator: Publisher catalogue; denominator is proprietary and subscription-dependent.

Access

  • verified working ICLR public site — tested 2026-07-25

Rights and constraints

  • Licence: Copyright/subscription/licence restrictions.
  • Fair use/rate/robots: Authenticated/subscription for much content; no bulk use assumed.

Known limitations

  • Not freely or wholly reusable; editorial treatment must not be represented as official court metadata.

Independent public prototype. Verify legal effect and current text against the cited official source. Model-assisted discovery metadata is not official legal classification or legal advice.
