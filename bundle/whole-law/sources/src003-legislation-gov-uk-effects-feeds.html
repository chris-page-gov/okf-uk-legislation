legislation.gov.uk effects feeds

Official editorial records of amendments and other effects; an effect record supports change research but absence is not proof of no effect.

Authority and coverage

  • Owner: The National Archives / King’s Printer
  • Authority: binding-authority
  • Source classes: SC05
  • Jurisdictions: United Kingdom, England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland
  • Coverage status: partially complete
  • Denominator: Official source describes category-specific partial coverage; denominator must be per type/period rather than global.

Access

Rights and constraints

Known limitations
