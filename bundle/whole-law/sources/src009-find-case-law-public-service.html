Find Case Law public service

Official free publication of judgments/decisions; precedential force depends on court, issue, date and later treatment.

Authority and coverage

  • Owner: The National Archives with MoJ, HMCTS and Judicial Office
  • Authority: binding-authority, persuasive-authority
  • Source classes: SC09, SC10, SC11, SC12, SC14
  • Jurisdictions: England and Wales, United Kingdom for UKSC/JCPC
  • Coverage status: partially complete
  • Denominator: Official courts-and-coverage pages provide per-court date ranges and, increasingly, counts/completeness descriptions.

Access

  • verified working Public website/search — tested 2026-07-25
  • verified working Courts and coverage — tested 2026-07-25

Rights and constraints

  • Licence: Open Justice Licence for ordinary reuse; computational analysis requires an additional licence.
  • Fair use/rate/robots: Reasonable requests; obey robots and no external indexing; programmatic bulk searching/enrichment is computational analysis requiring permission.

Known limitations

  • Not all decisions are written or sent; lower-court coverage is selective; appeal status may not be linked; no Scotland/NI corpus.

Independent public prototype. Verify legal effect and current text against the cited official source. Model-assisted discovery metadata is not official legal classification or legal advice.
